How reviews work

Manufacturers sometimes send me devices to test. When they do, the review says so. Before publishing, I share the review with the manufacturer so they can point out anything that's factually wrong. I correct inaccuracies; I don't change anything because they'd rather it said something different. I'm not paid for reviews.
